Chronicle

One living record of an event, from the first idea to the last lesson.

Today an exercise lives in pieces: a scenario document, a network build, injects in a spreadsheet, activity in logs, lessons in someone's notes. Chronicle keeps one record for the whole event, whether training, exercise or simulation. Apps publish what they built, did and saw, and subscribe to the parts they need. When the event ends, the record is the event: complete, attributable, and ready to seed the next one.
