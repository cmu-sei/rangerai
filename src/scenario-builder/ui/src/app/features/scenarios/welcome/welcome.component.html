Build the exercise around the training objective

A scenario defines what your team will practice, under what conditions and how you’ll judge the result.

It defines the network topology that range builders such as GraySpace can create or customize. The scenario gives GHOSTS agents their roles and tasks. For each exercise inject, you can choose who delivers it, when it reaches participants and what response evaluators should look for. Publish the plan to Chronicle so evaluators can compare what happened with the original objective.
